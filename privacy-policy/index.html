Privacy Policy

Mytheon Technologies — how we collect, use, and protect your data.

Effective date: 26 March 2026

1. Who We Are

Mytheon Technologies ("we", "our", or "us") is an AI-first technology company headquartered at 133/1 Gagarin Avenue, Almaty, Kazakhstan. We develop and operate AI-powered SaaS products including OpenEMDR.ai and AdsCopilot.ai.

This Privacy Policy explains how we handle personal information when you visit our website or use our products.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Contact information — name, email address, phone number, when you reach out to us directly.
  • Usage data — pages visited, time spent, browser type, and device information, collected automatically when you use our website.
  • Communications — messages you send us via WhatsApp or other channels.

We do not collect sensitive personal data (such as health or financial information) through this website.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your enquiries and provide customer support.
  • Improve our website and products based on usage patterns.
  • Send product updates or announcements if you have opted in.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

4. Cookies

Our website may use cookies to enhance your browsing experience. Cookies are small files stored on your device that help us understand how the site is used.

You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect some functionality of the site.

5. Data Sharing

We do not share your personal data with third parties except in the following circumstances:

  • Service providers — trusted partners who help us operate our website or products (e.g. hosting, analytics), bound by confidentiality agreements.
  • Legal requirements — if required by law or to protect our legal rights.

6. Data Retention

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this policy, or as required by law. When data is no longer needed, we securely delete or anonymise it.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your data.
  • Object to or restrict certain types of processing.
